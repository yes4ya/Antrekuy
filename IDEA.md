# AntrianOnline

A web-based digital queue management system designed for small businesses and clinics to eliminate physical waiting lines.

- Enable users to join a live queue remotely via a QR code or web link with estimated wait times.
- Provide a staff dashboard to call, skip, and manage customer tickets efficiently.
- Display real-time queue status on a public-facing display screen for waiting areas.
- Send automated SMS or WhatsApp notifications when a customer's turn is approaching.
